## ARCAID3 lexicon view (the one added code cell)

The next cell adds `current_frame.code`, `.objects`, `.native`, `.rules` and `.changes_since(...)` to the Python sandbox, lists them in the harness's prompt lines, adds "when to look" lines and a first-turn / new-level / `.rules` cue. It tests itself through the real sandbox and rolls back if anything fails. Version 2 adds a per-level scale lock; Version 3 adds rules learned from the level's steps; Version 4 adds the `.rules` cue and object permanence; Version 5 fixes 4-hex picture names and `.rules` timeout ordering; Version 6 adds multi-step contact permanence in `.code`/`.objects`/`.changes_since`, suppresses false `still` cues on read-only turns, filters mid-level death resets in `.rules`, and retains early scale-lock frames across >256 steps.
